## Scope & Phases Covered
- **Phase 01: Text Layout & Column Segmentation** — Continuous reading flow, two-column gutter separation, and margin wrap sanitization.
- **Phase 02: Rich Typography & List Hierarchies** — Font styling, super/subscripts, inline colors, and 7-level nested outline hierarchies.
- **Phase 03: In-Browser Table Reconstruction** — 1D coordinate clustering, merged headers, and cell border generation.
- **Phase 04: Bi-Directional Unicode & Structural Elements** — RTL Arabic/Hebrew shaping, Brahmic/CJK ligatures, callouts, and form fields.
- **Phase 05: High-Fidelity Smallpdf Parity & Reflow Engine** — Soft-line paragraph reflow, authentic table palettes, and exact Smallpdf structural parity.
- **Phase 06: Core WASM Vector & Layout Extractor** — Raw `OperatorList` graphics stream extraction for vector lines, rects, and fill/stroke RGBs.
- **Phase 07: Universal Intermediate AST & Multi-Format DLA** — Format-agnostic document tree decoupling layout extraction from target emitters.
- **Phase 08: High-Accuracy DOCX & Multi-Sheet XLSX Emitters** — Native OpenXML reflow and multi-sheet accounting spreadsheets with typed floats and accounting formats.
- **Phase 09: Editable PPTX Emitter & Web Worker Pipeline** — Native editable PowerPoint slides and non-blocking background async chunking pipeline.

## Audit Gap Closure
All 4 integration gaps identified during cross-phase tracing were resolved:
1. `pdfToPptx` upgraded from raster screenshot canvas to native editable 2D slides via `exportAstToPptx`.
2. `pdfToExcel` upgraded to route through `exportAstToXlsx` with formula-ready typed floats.
3. `pptxEmitter.js` completed with handlers for `NodeType.SHAPE` and `NodeType.CODE_BLOCK`.
4. Page conversion loops wrapped with `runChunkedConversion` in `conversionWorkerClient.js` for non-blocking UI.
